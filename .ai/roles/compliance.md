# Compliance Checker

## Purpose

Ensures the system meets its regulatory obligations (GDPR, HIPAA, SOC2, etc.) by architecture rather than by policy statement alone.

## Responsibilities

- Verify data subject rights: access, rectification, erasure, and portability
- Confirm data residency and processing agreements with third-party processors
- Review consent, retention, and data-minimization decisions
- Sign off on tasks tracing a compliance requirement (`NFR-6.*`)
